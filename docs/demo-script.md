# PRO•GO Colour Plan — 3-minute demo script

Live link: https://ariel-silvio.github.io/pro-go/ · QR: `docs/qr.png`
Prototype, simulated data, fictional characters. Student project for IE Business School. Not affiliated with or endorsed by L'Oréal.

## Two ways to run it

- **Live demo (you click):** menu (top right) → **Guided tour**. Next / Back move through the 10 steps below; each step highlights what to point at.
- **Recording (it plays itself):** open **https://ariel-silvio.github.io/pro-go/?demo=1**. About 3 minutes, same steps, same timings, price variant v1 every time. Pause with the ❚❚ button.

Before you start: menu → **Reset demo**, and check the last menu line shows the latest version.

## Click path (180 s)

| # | Time | Screen | Point at | Say (plain words) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:10 | S1 Invite | Salon Aurora header, Dana's card | "Maya's own salon invites her. The colourist she already knows comes to her home." |
| 2 | 0:10–0:18 | S2 Scan | The scan drawing | "It starts with a free hair scan. In the prototype it is simulated: no camera." |
| 3 | 0:18–0:35 | S3 Result | Score 58 and the four bars | "She gets a number she can see: 58 out of 100. It is not a medical measurement. The note comes from simple rules, not a model." |
| 4 | 0:35–1:00 | S4 Plan | The two plan cards, the small "v1" | "One price for a 90-day plan with two home visits. Each visitor sees one of three prices, so we can test what people would pay. 'Reserve my place' is a fake door: no payment." |
| 5 | 1:00–1:15 | S5 Booking | The chat confirmation | "She books the same colourist. The salon confirms by chat and reminds her about the patch test 48 hours before." |
| 6 | 1:15–1:30 | S6 Visit day | Patch test row, the 3-hour bar | "Safety first: no patch test, no colour. Scan, colour, treatment, re-scan, about three hours." |
| 7 | 1:30–1:50 | S7 Before and after | 58 → 66 | "After the visit the scan shows a change: 58 to 66. The next visit is booked before Dana leaves. That is our repeat-visit rate." |
| 8 | 1:50–2:10 | S8 Adjustment | The message | "And if the result is flat? The salon adjusts the treatment and the next scan is free. We keep the customer and the salon in charge." |
| 9 | 2:10–2:35 | Salon view | Alert, consent column, permit checkboxes | "The salon owner sees the alert. She can only invite clients who agreed, and only colourists with a home-service permit can go to homes. That is the Dubai permit risk." |
| 10 | 2:35–3:00 | CEO view | Targets, then the margin box as the price drops | "The CEO sees the targets for the Q4 2027 review. Our riskiest number is margin per visit: below about AED 375 a visit loses money. That is why we bundle colour, treatment and scan." |

## Honest lines to keep in

- "Everything you see is simulated: the scan, the AI note, the booking and the numbers."
- "Classmates are not our customers, so your answers are a rehearsal, not proof of demand."

## Recording tips

- Phone: open the `?demo=1` link, turn on screen recording, lock rotation.
- Laptop: a 1280 px wide window shows the phone and the "What you are seeing" panel. For a phone-only video, use Chrome DevTools device mode at 390 × 844.
- The auto-play always starts from a clean demo with AED 450/600 per visit (AED 900/1,200 per plan).

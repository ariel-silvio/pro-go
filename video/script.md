# 12 weeks with Layla — shooting script v1

Team D · L'Oréal Groupe innovation team · M4 (open innovation) · 8:00 · due Tue 10 Nov, upload by 20:00 CET
Draft 5 Nov 2026. Brackets `[ ]` are blanks to fill before recording. Grey notes for the editor are in *italics*.

**The idea in one line:** we never show Layla's face. We show her hands, her hair, her phone, her home. The audience fills in the rest, and the speakers on the right talk straight to the CEO.

**Format:** 16:9. Split screen during the chapters: Layla or the app on the left (960 × 1080), the speaker on the right. Full screen for the cold open, chapter cards, the score and the closing card. Every speaker looks screen-left, towards Layla, and turns to the lens for the one line marked **[LENS]**.

**Assumption for chapters 2–4:** `[PARTNER]` is a company that already runs licensed home beauty visits in Dubai (booking, routing, permits, insurance, payment). That is the one piece L'Oréal can't build fast. If the team picks a different kind of partner on Friday, chapters 2–4 change; the rest holds.

**Music:** one cue for the whole film. Sparse piano over a low string pad, warm, not corporate. It lifts once, at the week-12 re-scan, and drops out completely under the ask. *Descript stock library.*

**Why this order:** each chapter goes to the role that would own that answer at L'Oréal. Innovation names the gap, Technology picks the partner, Operations runs the collaboration, Marketing explains the value, Customer Experience runs the pilot through Layla's eyes, and Finance sets the money rules and makes the ask.

Each speaker: about 170 words, read calmly. **Time yourself: 65 to 75 seconds.** Under 62 s and you risk the 1-minute rule.

## CHAPTER 1 · WEEK 0 · The gap · 0:15–1:30 · Q1

**CARD** (3 s): Week 0 · The gap

**LEFT.** Layla clip 1 continues, then the app panel: her score, 58, and the four bars.
**ON SCREEN, left, once:** *Week 0.*

**AMJAD SAAD BENSALAMAH · Chief Innovation Officer** · lower third: *Amjad Saad Bensalamah* / *CHIEF INNOVATION OFFICER*

> Layla is made up. Everything about her is real.
>
> She's thirty-four, she lives in Dubai, and every eight weeks she colours her hair. Sometimes at the salon, sometimes with a box from the pharmacy. Both work. Neither tells her what's happening to her hair in between.
>
> That's what PRO•GO changes. A twelve-week plan. We measure her hair. A professional from her own salon comes to her home. Twelve weeks later, we measure again. If it didn't work, she sees it. So do we.
>
> L'Oréal can do most of this already. The science, the products, thousands of partner salons.
>
> What we can't do is the hard part in the middle. Getting a licensed professional into a private home in Dubai, on time, insured, with the right permit. A home visit there needs its own permit, tied to the person who walks through the door. We've never run that. And Layla's living room is not the place to learn.
>
> So we won't build it. **[LENS]** We'll bring in someone who already does it, every day.

## CHAPTER 3 · WEEK 2 · How we work together · 2:45–4:00 · Q3

**CARD** (3 s): Week 2 · How we work together

**LEFT.** Layla clip 3: gloved hands, a brush, colour going on. On the side table, a plain ivory kit and a small card: *Partner salon*. Slow drift, nothing hurried.
**ON SCREEN, left:** *My colourist. My living room.*

**OHAKAZILE MATHEBULA · Chief Operations Officer** · lower third: *Ohakazile Mathebula* / *CHIEF OPERATIONS OFFICER*

> Week two. Dana arrives. The same colourist Layla has trusted for years, now in her living room.
>
> Three partners make that hour happen, and each does only what it does best. The salon brings Dana and the relationship. L'Oréal brings the products, the measurement and the plan. [PARTNER] brings the visit itself: booking, route, permit, insurance, payment.
>
> The model is a paid pilot. Not an acquisition. Not a joint venture. We pay [PARTNER] per visit, for ninety days. We ask one thing in return: in our two pilot communities, no colour service with another hair brand while the test runs.
>
> The data stays where it's created. Hair results belong to L'Oréal and the salon. Booking data belongs to [PARTNER]. We share what we need to run the pilot. Nothing more. Once a month, the three of us sit at one table and read the same numbers.
>
> If it works, we talk about a longer contract. Maybe more than that. **[LENS]** But only after the numbers give us the right to ask.

## CHAPTER 4 · WEEK 4 · Why everyone wins · 4:00–5:15 · Q4

**REBECCA FERNANDES · Chief Marketing & Creative Officer** · lower third: *Rebecca Fernandes* / *CHIEF MARKETING & CREATIVE OFFICER*

> Week four. Nothing happens in week four. Layla uses her kit, the colour holds, and she doesn't think about us once.
>
> That's exactly what we want. Because look at who's winning while she isn't looking.
>
> The salon keeps a client who could have drifted to a box from the supermarket. It earns on a visit it couldn't have made alone. Our target is ten percent more income for partner salons by 2030. And the professional stays theirs. We don't hire her. We never will.
>
> [PARTNER] gets something a startup can't buy. The name of L'Oréal behind it, and a door to salons it could never sign one by one.
>
> And L'Oréal learns. For the first time, we see what our products do to real hair, in real homes, over twelve weeks. Not in a lab. Not in a survey. That is worth more than the margin on any single visit.
>
> **[LENS]** Three partners, three different reasons to want this to work. That's why it holds together.

## CHAPTER 5 · WEEK 8 · The pilot · 5:15–6:30 · Q5a

**CARD** (3 s): Week 8 · The pilot

**LEFT.** Layla clip 4: from behind, by the window, brushing. The light catches the shine. Then the app panel (p4): before 58, after 66, "Illustrative".
**ON SCREEN, left:** *Week 8. Second visit.*

**SAJA ALOWAYSHIQ · Chief Customer Experience Officer** · lower third: *Saja AlOwayshiq* / *CHIEF CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE OFFICER*

> Week eight. Second visit, second scan. This is where a pilot either teaches you something or just spends your money.
>
> Here is ours. Dubai. Two communities, Downtown and Business Bay. Three to five partner salons. Ninety days, from the second quarter of 2027.
>
> No app at the start. We match each client to a professional by hand, and that's on purpose. The AI comes after the test proves people want this. Not before. By hand, every mistake is one we can see.
>
> One person owns it: our Chief Operations Officer. Not a committee. One name, with the salons, the budget and the authority to stop it.
>
> Every week we watch three numbers. How many clients book a second visit. Whether each visit makes money after we pay the salon and [PARTNER]. And whether the hair score actually moves.
>
> Layla went from fifty-eight to sixty-six by week eight. She's an example, so that number is illustrative. **[LENS]** The real numbers come from the pilot. That's the whole reason to run it.

## CHAPTER 6 · WEEK 12 · The rules, and our ask · 6:30–7:50 · Q5b

**CARD** (3 s): Week 12 · The rules, and our ask

**LEFT.** Layla clip 5: two hands, the phone, a number rising. Then **full screen**: the score graphic, 58 to 74, "Illustrative". The music lifts here, once. Back to split for the rules.
**ON SCREEN, left:** *Week 12. 74. She renewed.*

**IGNACIO MONTERO ALCANTARA · Chief Financial Officer** · lower third: *Ignacio Montero Alcantara* / *CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER*

> Week twelve. Layla scans again. Seventy-four. And she renews, without anyone asking her to.
>
> One Layla proves nothing. Three hundred would. That's our target for 2027.
>
> So we write the rules now, before we fall in love with the idea.
>
> We scale if at least forty percent of clients book again, and every visit makes money. Then the next seven million euros are released at the end-2027 gate, and we add salons.
>
> We adapt if people come back but the money doesn't work. We change the price, the services, or the deal with [PARTNER].
>
> We stop if fewer than [one in four] come back. No second try. We keep what we learned and close it. Stopping early is cheaper than being right too late.
>
> *(Music drops out.)*
>
> **[LENS, from here to the end]** So this is our ask. Not a big bet. Ninety days in Dubai, inside the three million euros already planned. [PARTNER] at the door, the salon in the room, and your support when the first numbers come in.
>
> Give us twelve weeks. Layla already did.

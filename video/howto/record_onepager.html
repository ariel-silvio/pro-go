L'ORÉAL GROUPE  ·  TEAM D  ·  M4 VIDEO

Record your part

12 weeks with Layla  ·  about 70 seconds each  ·  one take you are happy with

1

Set up

  • Rear camera, landscape
  • Phone at eye height, about 1 m away
  • Sit in the centre of the frame
  • Plain dark top, no logos
2

Light & sound

  • Face a window, ceiling lights off
  • Quiet room; laptop and fans away from the phone
  • Wired or clip-on mic if you have one
3

Look

  • Look a little to your right: a post-it 25 cm right of the lens
  • Turn to the lens only on your LENS line
4

Read

  • Open the teleprompter on a laptop or tablet, right of the lens
  • Pick your name, press Start
  • Edit text if a word does not fit you
ariel-silvio.github.io/
pro-go/teleprompter.html
5

Record

  • Send a 10-second test to the group first
  • Wait 3 seconds before you speak and after you stop
  • Aim for 65–75 s; calm pace
6

Send

  • Upload the original file to Descript › Speakers
  • or a Google Drive link set to Anyone with the link
  • Name it M4_S2_Ariel_take1

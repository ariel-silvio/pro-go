# Partner salons to approach first (pilot shortlist, not in the film)

The film keeps the fictional Salon Aurora and Dana: naming real salons as "partner salons" would imply they have agreed.
This list is for the Miro board / report, labelled **shortlist to approach**. The pilot runs in **Downtown Dubai and Business Bay** (Saja, chapter 5), so the first wave is chosen there.
Suggested by Rebecca (8 Nov) and completed by a web search on 8 Nov 2026. Directory listings only: **call each salon to confirm** they do colour, have women colourists who could do home visits, and what they would need from a partner.

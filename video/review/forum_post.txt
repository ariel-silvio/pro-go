Team D – M4 video: 12 weeks with Layla
https://youtu.be/T2k37jMMXo4

Our recommendation to L'Oréal: a 90-day venture-client pilot in Dubai. L'Oréal Professional Products buys the home-visit service from ServiceMarket and works through partner salons. It doesn't build a new platform and it doesn't acquire one. The money comes from the €3m already planned, with a go or stop decision at the Q4 2027 review.

We tell it through Layla, a fictional client. Over 12 weeks, her own colourist visits her at home twice and a hair scan shows what changed. Each chapter is one week of her story, and the last one sets the rules we would use to stop early.

Presenters: Amjad Bensalamah, Ariel de Silvio, Qhakazile Mathebula, Rebecca Fernandes, Saja Alowayshiq and Ignacio (Nacho) Alcantara.

The prototype is linked in the video description. Layla, her voice and her scenes are AI-generated, and the data is simulated.
